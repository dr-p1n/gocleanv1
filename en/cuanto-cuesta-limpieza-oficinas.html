How much does office and commercial cleaning cost in Panama?

The cost of office and commercial cleaning in Panama depends on the size and use of the facility, the number of occupants and restrooms, the frequency, the service hours, the surfaces involved, and whether supplies are included.

A reliable quote has to assess the actual scope of work, not just apply a rate per square metre. This guide explains how recurring commercial cleaning is priced for offices, corporate buildings, clinics, schools, banks, and other facilities in Panama — and what to compare between providers before you sign.

What determines the price of a cleaning service?

The main factors behind any quote are:

  • The size and layout of the facility
  • The number of occupants and restrooms
  • How many days per week the service runs
  • The service hours per shift
  • The types of surfaces to be cleaned
  • How many common areas there are
  • The working hours available for the crew
  • Whether special products, tools, or equipment are needed

Why isn't a price per square metre enough?

A rate per square metre can be a useful reference for one-off jobs, but for recurring cleaning it does not, on its own, reflect the operational workload.

Two 250 m² offices can require very different shifts: one may have eight staff and a single restroom, while the other takes in thirty people and has three restrooms, a reception, meeting rooms, and a kitchen.

That is why square metres, headcount, layout, and how intensively the space is used all need to be assessed together.

How many cleaning hours does an office need?

As a general reference:

  • A small, low-traffic office may need around 2 hours a day.
  • Many small and mid-sized offices are covered in 4 hours.
  • High-traffic facilities may need 8 hours to keep restrooms, waste, kitchens, and common areas under control.

The right shift length should be confirmed with a site assessment; these figures are starting points, not a standard that fits every case.

What should a professional cleaning quote include?

A professional quote should spell out:

  • The staff assigned and the service hours
  • The tasks and how often each one is performed
  • How the service is supervised
  • The procedure for absences and replacements
  • Who supplies the products, bags, tools, and equipment
  • Which specialised work or sanitary consumables are quoted separately

Hiring someone to perform cleaning tasks is not the same as contracting a managed cleaning service. The latter brings supervision, coordination, follow-up, continuity, and cover when someone is absent or something goes wrong, within the agreed scope. When what you actually need is the staffing and its administration, that is structured as staff outsourcing.

Service with or without supplies: which one suits you?

In a service without supplies, the client provides the products, bags, and tools; in a service with supplies, the provider manages them, which takes the purchasing burden off the client and makes the methodology more consistent.

In a service with supplies, the provider manages the products and tools listed in the proposal.

The quote should distinguish cleaning products, such as detergents and disinfectants, from sanitary consumables, such as toilet paper, hand towels, and hand soap.

Why are some quotes so much cheaper than others?

A cheaper proposal may leave out supplies, equipment, supervision, or replacements. Compare the full scope: tasks, frequency, hours, supervision, absence cover, supplies, incident response, and follow-up.

What information should you provide to get a quote?

To be quoted accurately, it helps to provide:

  • The location of the facility
  • Approximate square metres
  • Number of occupants and restrooms
  • Common areas
  • Type of flooring
  • Desired frequency and the hours available

Frequently asked questions about commercial cleaning in Panama

Can I contract cleaning for only a few days a week?

Yes. Some offices do not need daily attention and work perfectly well with two or three visits a week. The right frequency depends mainly on how many people use the space and how heavily the restrooms, kitchens, and common areas are used.

Why is a site visit worth doing before quoting?

It allows the provider to check the layout, access points, floors, glass, carpets, and any special areas, and from there estimate hours and resources far more accurately.

Should a cleaning company provide replacements?

A provider of recurring services should have a mechanism to cover holidays, sick leave, and absences. This is one of the important differences between hiring an individual and contracting a managed operation.

Which services are quoted separately?

Deep carpet cleaning, hard-to-reach glass, and other non-routine work are usually scheduled and quoted separately.

How do I know whether my company needs 2, 4, or 8 hours of cleaning?

The best way is to assess size, number of occupants, restrooms, frequency, and the level of attention required. A site visit makes it possible to settle on a realistic shift and avoid contracting either too few hours or more than you need.

Are products and consumables included?

It depends on the contract. The proposal should state who supplies the products, bags, and tools, and should list sanitary consumables separately.
